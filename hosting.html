Hosting & security

On your premises or in the cloud.

The hospital chooses where its data lives. The software is the same either way, and staff use it in a web browser on desk computers and tablets, with nothing to install.

On your premises

Ophia runs on a server inside the hospital, and patient data never leaves the building.

  • Backups to the hospital's own storage
  • Works across the hospital network even when the internet is down
  • Suits hospitals that want records on site

In the cloud

We run it for the hospital, so there is no server to buy or look after.

  • Reachable from every branch
  • Updates without a site visit
  • Suits groups with several locations

On any screen

Staff sign in through a web browser on any desk computer or tablet. Patients use the app on their phones.

  • Each role signs in to its own portal
  • Phones add photos of paper records by QR code
  • Built on Django, Next.js and PostgreSQL

Security and safety

Checks are built into the work, not left on a clipboard.

Each role sees its own work

Portals and permissions follow the job. Ward nurses can write the operative notes, but only the operating surgeon can change the case itself.

Records lock after their editing window

Once the window closes, a clinical record can only be reopened by an administrator, who must give a reason.

Surgery waits for the checks

The theatre checklist opens only after the ward checklist is signed, and a case without signed consent cannot start.

An audit trail of changes

Changes to records are logged with who made them and when.

Phone links expire

Each QR upload link belongs to one patient and lasts 15 minutes. Photos go into the record, not into a chat on a staff phone.

Patients see only what's released

Reports reach the patient app only after the doctor has reviewed and released them, and family members need the patient's code.

Your data stays yours

A standard database, no lock-in.

Ophia keeps its records in PostgreSQL, the widely used open-source database. The hospital can export its data at any time, without needing us to read it.
