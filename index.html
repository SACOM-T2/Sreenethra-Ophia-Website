Follow a patient

From the first booking to the follow-up, on one record.

Each desk has its own screen, built around its own work, and sees what the last desk recorded. Pick a step or let it play. Move over a screen to look closer, and click to enlarge it.

The Ophia app

Patients see their visit as it happens.

The app runs on the same record the hospital uses, so what the doctor writes is what the patient sees.

  • Book and pay online with the doctor and slot they choose.
  • Their place in the queue on the day, step by step.
  • Prescriptions with eye-drop instructions, and reports once the doctor releases them.
  • Family access to look after a parent's or child's record.
